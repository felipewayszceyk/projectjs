Why people move to Ireland

01

A gateway to Europe

English-speaking, EU-adjacent and just a short flight from most of mainland Europe, Ireland is a popular first stop for people relocating from outside the continent.

02

A strong job market

Dublin in particular hosts European headquarters for major tech, pharma and finance companies, which means a steady stream of visa-sponsored roles.

03

Students and families welcome

From graduate visas to a well-regarded education system, Ireland attracts students and families looking for a fresh start.

04

About the weather

Mild all year round, rarely extreme — but pack a proper raincoat. You'll thank us later.

Explore where to live

Six Irish cities and towns, compared side by side.

DUBLIN

Dublin

Leinster (East Coast)

The capital and the country's economic engine. Home to European HQs for tech and pharma companies, a huge student population, and the best transport links — but also the highest rents in the country.

Population — 1.4M (greater area) Avg. rent — €1,900/month (1-bed, city centre)
Career & Nightlife Student-Friendly
CORK

Cork

Munster (South)

Ireland's second city: a walkable, food-loving town with a growing tech scene, easy access to the coast, and a noticeably calmer pace of life than Dublin.

Population — 220k Avg. rent — €1,500/month (1-bed, city centre)
Family-Friendly Budget-Friendly
GALWAY

Galway

Connacht (West Coast)

A compact, artsy university town on the Atlantic coast, known for festivals, live music and being minutes away from some of the country's best scenery.

Population — 80k Avg. rent — €1,400/month (1-bed, city centre)
Student-Friendly Family-Friendly
LIMERICK

Limerick

Munster (Mid-West)

A riverside city with a medieval core, one of the more affordable options among Ireland's cities, and a solid base for manufacturing and tech jobs.

Population — 95k Avg. rent — €1,300/month (1-bed, city centre)
Budget-Friendly Family-Friendly
WATERFORD

Waterford

Munster (South-East)

Ireland's oldest city, famous for crystal and a relaxed South-East coast lifestyle. Rents here are among the lowest of any Irish city.

Population — 60k Avg. rent — €1,150/month (1-bed, city centre)
Budget-Friendly Family-Friendly
KILKENNY

Kilkenny

Leinster (South-East)

A postcard-medieval town, less than 1.5 hours from Dublin, popular with families who want small-town life without being far from the capital.

Population — 27k Avg. rent — €1,200/month (town centre)
Family-Friendly Budget-Friendly
